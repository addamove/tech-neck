# Hands artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-11, using the existing exercise styles and achievement medals as visual references. No downloaded stock artwork is used.

The shared brief requested a two-column, five-row atlas of isolated hand and forearm demonstrations on a white background, consistent wrist anchors, turquoise cuffs, natural hand anatomy, no text and no baked-in arrows. Rows show fingers together/spread, open/curled fingers, supported wrist up/down, palm up/down, and thumb/little-finger side movements. Photo variants use an adult male or female hand. The marker variant uses bold black contours, simple peach crayon fill and flat turquoise cuffs to match the existing marker drawings.

All three accepted sources are 1024×1536. The last row was corrected with the built-in image editor so both poses show the same left hand, with the thumb on the same side. Only that row from the accepted correction is composed into the original atlas; the first four rows remain unchanged. Original and corrected PNGs are preserved privately in ignored `work/hands-art-originals/`.

`scripts/prepare-hands-art.mjs` compresses photos at WebP quality85 and the marker atlas strictly losslessly, preserving dimensions. It verifies decoded marker RGBA equality and writes measured row boundaries and wrist translations to `src/components/handsArtLayout.json`. `HandsArt.tsx` clips the source cell before translating it in a fixed stage, then overlays code-native movement arrows, except for finger bends, which have no arrows. Spread, finger bends and wrist bends map the first configured pose to the right column; palm turns and side-to-side map it to the left column. Small source differences in forearm shape remain; artwork is not stretched or redrawn to force an exact silhouette match.

The four-medal brief requested soft 3D teal, mint and warm-gold collectibles matching the existing achievement set: a caring hand, high five, paired helping hands, and a hand with a calendar. A targeted revision corrected the high-five fingers. The accepted 1254×1254 atlas is split into four 627×627 WebP medals: `hands-first`, `hands-five`, `hands-ten`, and `hands-days-7`. Titles, conditions and progress are rendered by the application; the artwork contains no text and awards no extra XP.

Movement references: [South Tees wrist range of movement](https://www.southtees.nhs.uk/resources/wrist-range-of-movement/), [Cambridge University Hospitals active wrist exercises](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/hand-therapy-active-wrist-exercises/), [Kent Community Health wrist range of motion](https://www.kentcht.nhs.uk/leaflet/wristactionrangeofmotion/), and [Mersey Care carpal tunnel guide](https://www.merseycare.nhs.uk/patient-leaflets/carpal-tunnel-syndrome-guide). The app's 36-second movement and 15-second preparation timings are a product design choice, not a clinical protocol. This routine offers gentle mobility without a carpal tunnel prevention or treatment claim.
