# Neck level 2 artwork

The app uses three styles (`male`, `female`, `marker`) for gentle head turns and arm circles. Each source atlas has three columns and two rows: head turns to the subject's left, center, and right in the first row; three phases of small arm circles in the second row. The same arm frames play for both directions; separate SVG arrows show forward and backward movement.

`node scripts/prepare-neck-level2-art.mjs --male SOURCE --female SOURCE --marker SOURCE --prompt PROMPT_FILE` preserves original sources in ignored `work/neck-level2-art-originals/`, records SHA-256 hashes and output dimensions in its manifest, and crops five native frames from each atlas (one third of its width, with the row split measured at native y=700). There is no raster resizing. Photographic styles use WebP quality 85. Marker crops use lossless WebP and the script verifies every decoded RGBA pixel against its source crop.

The deliverable files live under `public/art/neck-level2/STYLE/`: `head-turn-left.webp`, `head-turn-center.webp`, and `arm-circle-1.webp` through `arm-circle-3.webp`. Existing exercise images remain independent. Movement arrows are drawn by the application, so no arrows are baked into the sources. Head turns use a short horizontal curve near the head; arm circles use small circular arrows near the arms and reverse their direction during the backward half.

The accepted photographic sources are `exec-52f2c8ec-3b15-4552-aaff-26b2ad7591a5.png` (male) and `exec-39513dc0-ad85-4f7c-aeeb-99e9fbccc449.png` (female). The marker source is `exec-7d160b35-fb4b-4bea-99ca-8b86e5622001.png`, generated with the built-in image generation tool. Its actual native dimensions are 1254×1254; head frames are 418×700 and arm frames are 418×554. The measured row boundary y=700 falls between the complete upper hands and the lower heads, preserving all anatomy without neighboring fragments. The app normalizes stage width to 512 while preserving each crop aspect ratio. Arm phases receive only native CSS translations: male 0/4/9px, female 0/1.5/7px, marker 0/2/9px. Head frames are centered from measured torso anchors; right turns mirror the centered left frame. For all styles, the right-turn view mirrors the accepted left-turn frame and arrow in CSS. The unused right-turn cell is not shipped, preventing separate pose registration drift and avoiding duplicate bytes.

Prompt gist: a square sheet with six equal cells, three columns and two rows, white background, no text, grid, or baked arrows. Use the existing male/female/marker identity, upper body to the waist, fixed front camera and body registration. Top row: turn the head left, face forward, turn the head right, keeping the shoulders still. Bottom row: three small arm circle phases with both straight arms 15 degrees below horizontal, at shoulder height, and 15 degrees above horizontal; keep every hand inside its own cell. All poses retain consistent character, scale, anatomy, and torso placement.
